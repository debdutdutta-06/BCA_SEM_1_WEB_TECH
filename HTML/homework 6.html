INPUT, OUTPUT AND STORAGE DEVICES



INPUT DEVICES

Input devices are used to enter data or instructions into a computer.


  1. Keyboard – It is one of the most common and essential input devices used to enter data, text, and numbers into a computer. It is arranged in a specific Q W E R T Y layout and is used almost everywhere, from typing documents to entering commands, making it one of the most important devices for interacting with a computer.
    • It has alphabet keys.
    • It has function keys.
    • It has numeric keys, and many more.

  2. Biometric scanner – It is a security based input device that verifies a person physically, such as through face locks, fingerprints, or eye scans, instead of relying on passwords. It captures unique physical features of a person and matches them with stored data to confirm identity. It is widely used in smartphones, banks, offices, and airports for secure access.

  3. Microphone – It is an input device used to capture sound or voice from the user and convert it into an electrical signal that the computer can process. It is commonly used for voice calls, recording audio, voice commands, and online meetings.
OUTPUT DEVICES

Output devices are used to display or present processed data to the user.


  1. Monitor – It is an output device that displays visual information such as text, images, and videos on a screen, allowing the user to see the output of the computer. Monitors come in different types and sizes, and are one of the most essential parts of a computer system since almost all output is viewed through them.
    • LED
    • LCD
    • TOUCH SCREEN

  2. Speaker– It is an output device that converts electrical signals from the computer into sound waves, allowing the user to hear audio output such as music, voice, or system notifications. Speakers are widely used in computers, laptops, and multimedia systems for entertainment and communication.

  3. Printer – It is a very useful output device that is now found in almost every institute, office, and home. It helps to get a hard copy (physical/paper copy) of documents, images, or reports, and can print in both colored and black-and-white forms depending on the type of printer used.

STORAGE DEVICES

Storage devices are used to save and store data, files, programs, and information.



  1. SSD (Solid State Drive) – It is a modern day storage device that uses flash memory chips instead of spinning disks to store data. Because of this, it is one of the fastest ways to store and access data. SSDs are also more reliable and durable than HDDs because they have no moving parts, and they consume less power while being significantly faster.

  2. Memory Card (SD Card) – It is a small, lightweight, and easily portable flash storage device used to store data such as photos, videos, and files. Nowadays, it is commonly used in cameras, mobile phones, tablets, and many other electronic devices due to its compact size and ease of use.

  3. Pen Drive (USB Flash Drive) – It is also a small, portable, and removable storage device that uses flash memory technology to store data. It connects to a computer or laptop through a USB port and is widely used to transfer files between devices or to carry extra storage conveniently wherever needed.
